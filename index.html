Your Guide

Hi, I'm Mikuri 👋

I was born in Okinawa and moved to Tokyo a year ago. To make friends in my new city, I went everywhere — little solo adventures through every corner of town. Along the way I collected my favorite discoveries: local places that aren't on the map, the kind of spots no guidebook will ever show you. Those are exactly the places I want to share with you.

I love showing visitors the city behind the guidebooks — the 6-seat bars, the backstreet shrines, the food counters with no English menu and no tourists.

I started My Tokyo because the best moments of travel happen in places you'd never enter alone. My job is to open those doors, handle every bit of Japanese for you, and send you home with stories (and a map of my favorite spots for the rest of your trip).

— See you in Tokyo! 🏮

Frequently Asked Questions

Is everything in English?
Yes! All tours are run in English, and we'll happily teach you a few fun Japanese phrases along the way.
Are vegetarian or non-alcoholic options available?
Yes. Vegetarian options are available with advance notice — please mention dietary restrictions (allergies, religious requirements) in the booking form. Non-drinkers are very welcome: soft drinks can replace any included alcoholic drinks.
How do I pay?
Payment is by credit card in advance. After you send a booking request, we'll email you a secure payment link — your reservation is confirmed once payment is completed.
What is the cancellation policy?
Free cancellation up to 7 days before the tour date. Cancellations within 7 days of the tour: 50% of the tour price. Cancellations on the tour day or no-shows: 100%.
What happens if it rains?
Tours run rain or shine. We'll adjust the itinerary where needed and recommend bringing rain gear on wet days.
Can children join the tours?
It depends on the tour — age requirements are listed in each tour's details. Tours that visit bars are for guests aged 20 and over (Japan's legal drinking age); sightseeing tours are family-friendly and welcome all ages.
Where do we meet?
Each tour's meeting point is listed in its tour details in the booking section. The exact meeting spot is shared after your booking is confirmed.
