Bakery コムギノホシ (Komugi no Hoshi): Choosing Bread by the Shop's Own Ranking

I couldn't decide what to get, so I followed the shop's ranking: a mentaiko French bread and a beef curry bun for ¥708. Four minutes from Shindaita Station.

MikuriLocal guide in Tokyo · Published Nov 25, 2026

There were so many kinds of bread at Bakery コムギノホシ (Komugi no Hoshi) that I couldn't decide what to get. So I let the shop decide for me and picked from its popularity ranking.

The bakery is in Hanegi, in Setagaya. The closest station is Shindaita, about four minutes on foot. From Shimokitazawa Station it's about fifteen minutes.

What I ate

I took No. 2 and No. 3 on the shop's ranking:

  • Hakata mentai France (博多明太フランス), No. 2: French bread with mentaiko, a spicy cod roe. ¥358 before tax, ¥386 with tax.
  • Beef curry bun (牛肉ゴロッとカレーパン), No. 3: a curry bun with big chunks of beef. ¥298 before tax.

I was hungry when I walked in, but these two filled me up.

My tip

Can't decide? Look for the ranking cards on the shelves and start from the top.

So many kinds

Sausage breads, cream buns, sandwiches, and cute ones with panda faces and cat faces. There were plenty more I wanted to try.

When I went, the shop had an autumn fair with limited-time breads, including pumpkin ones.

The shop

The shop is in a concrete building, with a tree growing by the steps. The sign out front says Bread Coffee Beer.

There are terrace seats right in front of the shop.

At the kitchen-side entrance, you can see into the kitchen through the glass door.

What it cost

Two breads came to ¥708 with tax.

Should you go?

I want to go back. There were still so many breads I wanted to try.
